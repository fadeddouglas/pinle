Privacy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Pinle is a daily closest-to-the-pin game. You can play on the web at pinle.io or in the Pinle app. You do not need an account.

Your shots and settings stay on this device in local browser storage. We do not have a login, and we do not collect a name, email, or score history on a server.

The website may use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. The native app does not include that tracker. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

Share cards you send through Messages, Instagram, or other apps leave the device only because you chose to send them.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/fadeddouglas/pinle or write hello@pinle.io.
