Support

Pinle is a daily par 3. You get three shots. Closest to the pin wins the day. A new hole appears each UTC day.

Play in a browser at pinle.io or in the Pinle app.

  • Scores stay on this device. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app resets them.
  • Need three fresh shots? Wait for the next UTC day.
  • Share uses your phone’s share sheet so you can send the card to Messages, Instagram, and other apps.

Bugs or help: GitHub issues or hello@pinle.io.
